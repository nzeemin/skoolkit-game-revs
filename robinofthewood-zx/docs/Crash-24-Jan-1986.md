# Source: CRASH Christmas Special 1985/#24 (Jan 1986), Robin of the Wood review

OCR'd from the original magazine review/preview (pp.10-12). Kept here for the
canonical character/item terminology and stated game mechanics it confirms - used
to cross-check and name things in `Actors.md`/`General.md`/`Room-Format.md` and

## Story and setup

Robin, son of Aleric, is keeper of the Arrow (the "Shaft of Power") - a silver arrow
symbolising freedom to the Saxons, taken by the **Sheriff of Nottingham** after
arranging Aleric's death. Years later the Sheriff stages an archery contest with the
arrow as prize, expecting Robin (now a Saxon folk hero) to enter, and sends Norman
knights to hunt him in the wood. The player's goal: recover the arrow by winning the
contest at the Sheriff's castle - but first complete several tasks in the forest.

Three areas: the forest, the castle dungeons, and the castle itself. 330 screens.

## Characters and their confirmed roles

- **The Ent** ("remember them from Lord of the Rings?") - an old wise tree-being who
  holds Robin's bow, sword and three magic arrows. To get them, Robin must give the
  Ent **three bags of gold per weapon**.
- **Bishop of Peterborough** - the "Evil Bishop" who possesses that gold, guarded by
  an escort of crossbow-wielding Normans. Some fighting is needed before he can be
  robbed.
- **Sheriff of Nottingham** - patrols/appears in the wood; if he spots Robin he is
  imprisoned in the castle dungeons (escape needs a key).
- **Witches** - materialise from time to time while travelling to the castle. Give a
  witch the right number of flowers and she may help (transport Robin elsewhere);
  give her too few (or none) and she sends Robin to the castle dungeon - and, per the
  reviewer, "nicks your money bags" if you have none to give.
- **Hermit doctor** - heals Robin if injured, but "isn't too nice" if Robin is
  carrying weapons at the time.
- **Boars** - tusked; contact injures Robin.
- **Norman soldiers** - patrol the forest and "shoot on sight."

## Items and mechanics

- **Weapons**: Robin starts with a quarterstaff (hand-to-hand only, "perfectly
  adequate for despatching the Normans"). The bow, sword and three magic arrows come
  from the Ent, each costing three bags of gold.
- **Magic arrows**: while any remain, Robin isn't recognised by the Sheriff; once the
  last one is used, he can be spotted and must escape.
- **Flowers**: currency for witches - carry enough or risk the dungeon (or losing
  money bags).
- **Key**: needed to escape the castle dungeons.
- **Extra lives**: scattered in the forest; Robin has only one life at game start.
- **Energy/antlers**: Robin's health is shown as a pair of antlers that change colour
  with energy level; carried objects and extra lives are displayed just below them.
  Severe wounds kill Robin (one life at the start).

## Reviewer notes (colour on presentation, not mechanics)

- Compared favourably to Odin's previous game, *Nodes of Yesod*.
- Noted as visually similar to *Sabre Wulf* at first glance, but "much more involved,"
  with character interaction playing a bigger role.
- Controls: definable keys; joystick Kempston/Protek/Cursor/Interface 2.
- Scores: Use of computer 89%, Graphics 95%, Playability 96%, Getting started 88%,
  Addictive qualities 94%, Value for money 90%, Overall 94%.
